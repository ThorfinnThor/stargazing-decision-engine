## Method and limitations

- Used the live Amazon.de browser UI. Search results were screened for exact model identity, then candidate detail pages were opened. For unsuccessful searches, a second query or broader model-family search was checked.
- Inspected selected variants, product information and buying controls. Opened the Mountain Pass seller drawer because a product page alone did not establish an orderable offer.
- The anonymous browser's existing delivery location was Ulm 89077. Actual eligibility, shipping dates and sellers can differ by delivery address and time. No checkout or purchase was performed.
- An Amazon listing is not image-reuse permission. This audit grants no approval to copy Amazon product photography or customer content.
- Seven matching offers are link candidates only. Activation still requires the user's Amazon marketplace/tracking configuration; no tracking ID was invented.

## Suggested follow-up

Use the seven matching offers as Amazon-link candidates. Keep the two ambiguous links inactive. For the seven products with no verified Amazon match, either retain editorial information without a buy button or select and research an actual replacement; do not point the existing product name at a different model. Removing items from comparison guides also requires updating the comparisons and conclusions.
