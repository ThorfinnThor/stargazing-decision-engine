## Revalidation rule

Direct cards are a curated layer, not a permanent inventory claim. Reopen the
canonical provider URL before each editorial review. Remove or replace an
offer when it redirects to a generic catalog, renders only a site shell, moves
outside the named destination, or changes from night astronomy to a different
activity. Rebuild the static redirect catalog after every change.
