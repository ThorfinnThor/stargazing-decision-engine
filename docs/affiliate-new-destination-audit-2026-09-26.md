# Affiliate activity audit for the 50 catalog additions

Review date: 2026-09-26

## Scope and publication rule

The 50 destinations added in the 100-to-150 catalog expansion were checked for current, destination-specific GetYourGuide and Viator products. AlUla already had a reviewed direct product before this pass. This review therefore covered the other 49 destinations.

Only an exact, currently reachable product page was accepted. Search pages, geographic name collisions, incidental drive-through stops, distant gateway-city products without a meaningful destination visit, and weak or misleading matches were rejected. A regional activity is labelled as such and must not imply a guided astronomy experience. Affiliate availability does not validate public access to an observing location.

## Rejected examples worth retaining for later reviews

- Big Cypress: a long self-guided audio-tour listing was too broad and weakly reviewed to recommend as a destination activity.
- Kopaonik: a category result referenced a day tour, but no reliable current exact product page could be verified.
- Attersee-Traunsee: the available match treated Attersee as a brief incidental stop rather than the destination.
- Grand Canyon-Parashant: a South Rim product appeared under the wrong geography and was rejected.
- Aigüestortes, Vercors, Lassen Volcanic, and Cedar Breaks: category results existed, but no suitable exact product link was verified for this release.
