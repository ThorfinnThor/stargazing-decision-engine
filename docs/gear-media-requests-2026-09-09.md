# Remaining gear images — manufacturer follow-up

Checked 9 September 2026. **23 images remain published; no additional images approved in this follow-up. All 16 remaining products still need a usable, exact-model asset with documented permission.**

The remaining products belong to 13 manufacturers. Official product photos and press downloads were investigated, including the Anker media portal in the browser. Finding an image URL is not recorded as permission to republish. This is a conservative publishing decision, not a legal determination that all editorial reuse is forbidden.

## New findings

- Anker's official media library lists the exact C300 DC and seven white-background images. The download controls did not deliver a usable file in this session. The C300 and C300 DC have separate records; they must not be confused.
- Jackery's German newsroom has a product-scene download, but it resolves to an Explorer 1000 instruction manual rather than Explorer 300 Plus photos.
- Petzl's press link leads to a dealer login. No new accounts were created or access restrictions bypassed.
- The existing Astroshop approval does not by itself establish permission for photos taken from another manufacturer's site.
