# GetYourGuide audit of 57 uncovered destinations

Audit date: 2026-09-07

## Scope and result

All 57 destinations without a published stargazing offer at the start were researched. Seven verified direct products were added to their destination and location-tour pages in English and German. Coverage rises from 18 to 25 of 75 destinations (31 astronomy offers, 90 regional offers). Fifty destinations still have no verified published astronomy product. This is a research outcome, not a claim that no operator exists.

## Method and limits

Destination-specific GetYourGuide web searches used the destination name with stargazing, astronomy or night-tour terms, with geographical disambiguation where needed. Candidate descriptions were checked for a real local observing component. Travel articles, category pages, unrelated regions and incidental sunset/camping copy did not qualify. The eight strongest direct candidates were opened in the browser with the complete affiliate URL. Seven resolved to matching product pages and qualified; Jaisalmer resolved correctly but has conflicting product details.

The query labels below are reproducible search topics, not a verbatim search-engine transcript. No dates were booked and no commission attribution test was performed. Browser resolution establishes product identity, not future availability. Existing regional offers were preserved, not re-audited in this batch. No Viator offers or automatic widgets were enabled.

## Accepted product evidence

- **brecon-beacons** — [Stargazing at Brecon Beacons Observatory](https://www.getyourguide.com/brecon-l101511/brecon-group-stargazing-at-brecon-beacons-observatory-t1079824/?referral_redirect=1&partner_id=BKWM9K1&utm_medium=online_publisher&cmp=Stargazing). Full tracked URL resolved to this product; provider, programme and local fit checked. Join Dark Sky Wales for a 90-minute telescope session at the visitor centre near Libanus. Cloudy conditions mean an indoor astronomy talk instead; the listing is non-refundable, so read the weather terms before booking.
- **bryce-canyon** — [Telescope evening in Bryce Canyon City](https://www.getyourguide.com/garfield-county-utah-l171107/bryce-canyon-stargazing-experience-t1118327/?referral_redirect=1&partner_id=BKWM9K1&utm_medium=online_publisher&cmp=Stargazing). Full tracked URL resolved to this product; provider, programme and local fit checked. A 90-minute guided telescope session at a private observing site in Bryce Canyon City, rather than a National Park Service programme inside the park. Chairs and blankets are provided. Confirm the meeting point and any optional shuttle when booking.
- **capitol-reef** — [Capitol Reef night-photography workshop](https://www.getyourguide.com/en-gb/torrey-utah-l169831/capitol-reef-astro-photography-tour-t492007/?referral_redirect=1&partner_id=BKWM9K1&utm_medium=online_publisher&cmp=Stargazing). Full tracked URL resolved to this product; provider, programme and local fit checked. This three-hour outing teaches night-sky photography around Capitol Reef with pickup options in Torrey. Bring a charged camera and tripod. It is a photography workshop, not a telescope session; check the equipment requirements and meeting arrangements with the provider.
- **arches** — [Stargazing and photography at The Windows](https://www.getyourguide.com/moab-l1509/moab-the-windows-astro-photography-and-stargazing-hike-t597462/?referral_redirect=1&partner_id=BKWM9K1&utm_medium=online_publisher&cmp=Stargazing). Full tracked URL resolved to this product; provider, programme and local fit checked. Meet at The Windows in Arches National Park for a two-hour night hike and photography instruction. The route covers roughly two miles. Red headlamps and tripods are offered; arrange your journey to the trailhead and check park-entry requirements separately.
- **kaikoura** — [Kaikōura telescope tour with Moana Skies](https://www.getyourguide.com/kaikoura-l37419/kaikoura-explore-the-cosmos-stargazing-tour-t1269017/?referral_redirect=1&partner_id=BKWM9K1&utm_medium=online_publisher&cmp=Stargazing). Full tracked URL resolved to this product; provider, programme and local fit checked. Explore the southern sky on a 90-minute English-language telescope tour with Moana Skies. Blankets and hot chocolate are included. The observing location can change with the weather, and the provider may make the weather decision up to an hour before the start.
- **oudtshoorn** — [Guided telescope viewing in Oudtshoorn](https://www.getyourguide.com/oudtshoorn-l36046/oudtshoorn-celestial-stargazing-with-telescope-and-guide-t433401/?referral_redirect=1&partner_id=BKWM9K1&utm_medium=online_publisher&cmp=Stargazing). Full tracked URL resolved to this product; provider, programme and local fit checked. True South Travel runs this one-hour telescope session from the Karoo Stargazing hub at Rosedalia Farm Stall in Oudtshoorn. The provider confirms the meeting arrangements and expected weather before the activity. The objects visible depend on the date and conditions.
- **wadi-rum** — [Bedouin dinner and an evening under the stars](https://www.getyourguide.com/wadi-rum-village-l191012/wadi-rum-bedouin-dinner-and-stargazing-experience-t1100097/?referral_redirect=1&partner_id=BKWM9K1&utm_medium=online_publisher&cmp=Stargazing). Full tracked URL resolved to this product; provider, programme and local fit checked. Travel from Wadi Rum Village to Desert Bird Camp for dinner and a hosted evening looking at the stars. This three-hour experience includes return transport to the village, but no overnight stay. No telescope is advertised; expect skywatching and Bedouin stories rather than an instrument-led astronomy tour.
