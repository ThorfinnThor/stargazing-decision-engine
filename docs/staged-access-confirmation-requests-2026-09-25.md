# Staged destination access-confirmation requests

Status: prepared, not sent. Last checked: 2026-09-25.

These requests cover the seven staged destinations whose real ERA5, Black
Marble, Copernicus DEM and score snapshots are complete but whose safe,
permitted night-access facts still require a written first-party response.
They do not authorize publication or activation. Destination and site records
must remain inactive, and image records must remain pending, until the response
is reviewed and every other activation gate is complete.

## Response evidence to retain

Save the complete reply, its date, the responder's name and role, and any
attachment or linked rule page. A usable response must identify the venue or
route unambiguously and answer the applicable questions below. Marketing copy,
a room booking, a daytime visit or a map pin is not evidence of night access.

For every accepted venue, record:

- the exact named guest area that may be used after dark;
- whether access requires an overnight booking, guide, ranger or advance booking;
- permitted hours and any closing, gate or border constraints;
- arrival, parking, walking, vehicle and return instructions;
- weather, seasonal, wildlife, fire, lighting and movement restrictions;
- whether photography equipment and a tripod may be used quietly;
- a first-party coordinate or map reference for the approved footprint.

## Shared introduction

Use this introduction for each request and append only the destination-specific
questions below.

> Subject: Written access confirmation for a stargazing travel guide
>
> Hello,
>
> We maintain Stargazing Index, a bilingual travel-planning guide. We are
> reviewing a possible listing for your property or managed area. The listing is
> currently unpublished and we will not describe any place as accessible after
> dark without written first-party confirmation.
>
> Could you please answer the questions below for current operations and identify
> the staff member or authority responsible for the guidance? We will treat a
> booking, daytime access or a public map as insufficient unless you explicitly
> confirm the permitted night-time arrangement.

## Review and catalogue update procedure

For each reply:

1. Archive the full first-party response and record its date and responder.
2. Verify any coordinate against the named property or authority map without
   widening the allowed footprint.
3. Update `data-config/sources/observation-sites.json` only when the response
   establishes a reproducible site and safe access wording.
4. Add the response and resulting finding to
   `data-config/sources/staged-factual-reviews.json`; use `verified` only when no
   required factual change remains.
5. If a coordinate changes, regenerate the ERA5, Black Marble, Copernicus DEM
   and real-score snapshots at the new coordinate.
6. Rerun both staged audits and the full data validation, tests, typecheck and
   production build.
7. Do not change any `active` or image-licence status during this procedure.
